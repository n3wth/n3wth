## How to Use MOCs

- **Navigate**: Each MOC serves as a specialized index for its domain
- **Explore**: Follow links to dive deeper into specific topics
- **Connect**: Discover relationships between different knowledge areas
- **Grow**: MOCs evolve as new connections and insights emerge

> [!tip] Navigation Tips
>
> - MOCs are living documents that grow with your knowledge
> - Use backlinks to discover how topics connect across domains
> - Check MOCs regularly as they're updated with new insights

## Creating New MOCs

When a topic area becomes rich enough to warrant its own map:

1. Identify core themes and subtopics
2. Create logical groupings and hierarchies
3. Add contextual descriptions for each link
4. Connect to related MOCs and notes
