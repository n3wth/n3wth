# Events

Professional and personal events worth attending or remembering. Conferences, meetups, and gatherings that contribute to [[Career Development]] and [[Networking]].

## Types of Events

**Conferences**
Large-scale industry gatherings. Best for:

- Learning about broad trends
- Networking with diverse professionals
- Discovering new tools and approaches

**Meetups**
Local, focused gatherings. Best for:

- Deep dives into specific topics
- Building local professional community
- Low-commitment exploration of interests

**Workshops**
Hands-on learning sessions. Best for:

- Practical skill development
- [[Deliberate Practice]] with guidance
- Immediate applicability of learnings

## Event Philosophy

- **Quality over quantity**: Better to deeply engage with fewer events
- **Active participation**: Speak, ask questions, contribute—not just observe
- **Follow-up matters**: The real value often comes from post-event connections
- **Capture insights**: Take notes on key takeaways while they're fresh
