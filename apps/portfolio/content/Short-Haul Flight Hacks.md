# Short-Haul Flight Hacks

Short-haul regional flights often carry disproportionately high cash prices. Targeted partner award redemptions yield high cents-per-point (cpp) returns on regional routes.

## JetBlue TrueBlue Partner Sweet Spot

JetBlue TrueBlue features partner redemptions on United Airlines short-haul routes at fixed award rates:

- **Route Example**: SFO ↔ BUR (San Francisco to Burbank) on United flights.
- **Award Cost**: 10,000 TrueBlue points + $5.60 each way.
- **Transfer Mechanism**: 1:1 instant transfer from Chase Ultimate Rewards to JetBlue TrueBlue.
- **Valuation**: Yields ~2.8 cpp value against cash fares on peak West Coast short-haul dates.

## British Airways Avios for Regional Short-Hauls

British Airways operates a distance-based award chart, making Avios exceptionally valuable for short non-stop regional hops:

- **Distance Bands**: Flights under 650 miles start at 4,750 to 7,500 Avios.
- **European Hops**: Ideal for short positioning flights such as Paris (CDG/ORY) to London, Bristol, or Cardiff.
- **Transfer Sources**: Bilt Rewards and Chase UR transfer 1:1 to British Airways Avios.

## Airport Selection & Efficiency

- **West Coast Hubs**: Favor regional airports like Burbank (BUR) over Los Angeles International (LAX) for faster TSA throughput, closer gate proximity, and lower ground transport friction.
- **Alaska Mileage Plan**: Alaska Silver / Elite status provides complimentary upgrades and preferred seating on West Coast corridor flights.
