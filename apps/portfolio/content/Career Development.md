- Mentorship

- Building connections
- Industry events
- Professional groups

### 1. Career Planning

- Identifying opportunities
- Mapping career paths
- Setting milestones

### 2. Performance Improvement

- Feedback loops
- Self-assessment
- Skill application

- Be proactive
- Seek feedback
- Adapt to change
