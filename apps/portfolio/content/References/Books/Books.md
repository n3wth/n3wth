# Books

Reading is one of the highest-leverage learning activities. Books represent distilled expertise—years of someone's experience compressed into a few hours of reading time.

## Why Track Books?

- **Retention**: Writing about books dramatically improves what sticks
- **Recommendations**: Having a curated list ready when someone asks
- **Patterns**: Seeing what topics draw your attention over time
- **Connection**: Linking book ideas to personal projects and other notes

## Book Note Framework

For each book worth noting:

- **Core thesis**: What's the main argument or insight?
- **Key takeaways**: 3-5 ideas worth remembering
- **Personal application**: How does this connect to my life/work?
- **Quality rating**: Would I recommend this? To whom?

## Reading Philosophy

- **Abandon freely**: Life's too short for books that don't engage you
- **Re-read classics**: Great books reward multiple readings
- **Balance genres**: Mix fiction and non-fiction, deep and light
- **Apply actively**: Knowledge unused is knowledge lost
