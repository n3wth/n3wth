# Reading Goals 2024

A year of intentional reading—balancing depth with breadth, fiction with non-fiction.

## The Goals

- [ ] **12 books minimum** - One per month feels achievable while consuming plenty of articles
- [ ] **50/50 split** - Fiction for pleasure and perspective; non-fiction for growth
- [ ] **Write notes for each** - Focus on personal takeaways, not summaries
- [ ] **Connect to projects** - Link book concepts to real work

## Why These Goals?

Articles are easy. Books require commitment. This year is about rebuilding the habit of deep reading rather than constant content snacking.

## Areas to Explore

**Technical**

- System Design
- Software Architecture

**Personal Growth**

- Psychology
- Leadership
- Decision Making

**For Pleasure**

- Literary Fiction
- Science Fiction

## The Meta-Goal

The point isn't to hit a number—it's to become a more thoughtful reader who extracts real value from books rather than just consuming them.
