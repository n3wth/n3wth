# Gardening

Gardening is both a practical skill and a meditative practice. It teaches patience, observation, and the satisfaction of nurturing growth over time.

## Why Garden?

- **Fresh produce**: Nothing beats homegrown vegetables and herbs
- **Mental health**: Gardening is proven to reduce stress and anxiety
- **Connection to seasons**: Understanding natural cycles
- **Sustainability**: Reducing food miles and packaging waste
- **Physical activity**: Low-impact outdoor exercise

## Growing Philosophy

**Start small**: One successful container garden beats an ambitious plot that overwhelms you.

**Observe first**: Watch your space through seasons before major planting. Where does sun hit? Where does water collect?

**Fail forward**: Every dead plant teaches something. Keep notes on what works in your specific conditions.

## Indoor vs Outdoor

Both have their place. Indoor plants bring life to living spaces year-round. Outdoor gardens connect you to larger ecosystems and seasonal rhythms.
