# Open Questions

A living list of questions I'm actively curious about. Some may find answers, others may evolve into new questions. This is the engine of [[Active Learning]].

## Knowledge & Learning

- How do I balance depth vs breadth in learning?
- What's the optimal frequency for reviewing notes?
- How can I better measure learning progress?

## Career & Work

- What does meaningful work look like at different life stages?
- How do I build influence without formal authority?
- What skills will age well vs become obsolete?

## Health & Wellbeing

- What's the minimum effective dose for [[Health/Exercise|exercise]]?
- How do I build sustainable energy management habits?
- What environmental factors most affect focus?

## Systems & Productivity

- Is there a right amount of structure vs flexibility?
- How do I know when a system is helping vs hindering?
- What's worth optimizing vs what should I just accept?

## Philosophy

- How do I balance ambition with contentment?
- What does "enough" mean in different contexts?
- How do I make decisions under uncertainty?

---

> Questions are the beginning of knowledge. Answers are what we accumulate. But the best learning often leads to better questions.
