Cooking Methods encompass various techniques used to prepare and cook food, enhancing flavor and texture.

## Core Techniques

### Dry Heat

- Baking
- Roasting
- Grilling

### Moist Heat

- Boiling
- Steaming
- Poaching
- Sous vide (water-bath cooking)

### Combination

- Braising
- Stewing
