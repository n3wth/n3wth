# Podcasts

Podcasts transform dead time into learning opportunities. Commutes, workouts, and chores become chances to absorb new ideas.

## My Listening Philosophy

- **Curate ruthlessly**: Life is too short for mediocre content
- **Use variable speed**: 1.5-2x for familiar hosts, 1x for dense material
- **Take notes on gems**: Capture insights that resonate using [[Progressive Summarization]]
- **Follow curiosity**: Don't finish things that don't engage you

## Listening Categories

**Learning & Growth**
Deep dives into psychology, productivity, and personal development. Good for focused attention.

**Technology & Culture**
Staying current with tech trends and their societal implications.

**Interviews & Conversations**
Long-form discussions that reveal how interesting people think.

**Background Listening**
Lighter content for when multitasking or doing manual work.

## Discovery Methods

- Recommendations from trusted sources
- Guest appearances from known figures
- Curator playlists and "best of" lists
- Following up on book authors
