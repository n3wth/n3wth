# Actual Gardening Costs

Understanding the real costs of starting a garden (my newest hobby) can be challenging. While many "budget garden" guides exist online, I want to share my actual expenses to help others plan their garden projects. Here's a transparent breakdown of my investments in my San Francisco urban garden, starting from August 2024.

> Total cost to date: **$900**

My projects include automated irrigation, raised bed construction, and establishing both edible and ornamental areas. Though individual purchases seemed modest, the total cost reveals a different perspective. Fortunately, many items are one-time investments that will serve for years.
