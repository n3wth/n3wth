# Digital Gardening

Digital gardening is the practice of cultivating ideas in public, allowing them to grow and evolve over time. Unlike traditional blogging, digital gardens embrace imperfection and continuous refinement.

## Core Principles

- **Learning in public**: Share thoughts as they develop
- **Continuous growth**: Notes evolve from seeds to evergreen content
- **Non-linear exploration**: Create connections, not chronologies
- **Imperfection welcomed**: Published doesn't mean finished
