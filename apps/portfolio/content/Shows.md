# Shows

A collection of TV series and films that have left an impression—whether for their storytelling, craft, or ideas worth thinking about.

## Why Track What We Watch?

- **Recommendations ready**: When someone asks "what should I watch?"
- **Pattern recognition**: Understanding what resonates with you
- **Avoiding rewatches**: Unless intentional
- **Cultural literacy**: Staying connected to shared narratives

## Viewing Categories

**Currently Watching**
Active series and ongoing viewing.

**Completed**
Finished series and films—rated and reviewed.

**Want to Watch**
The queue of recommendations and discoveries.

**Abandoned**
Life's too short for shows that don't hold attention. No shame in stopping.

## Selection Philosophy

- Quality over quantity—better to deeply engage with fewer shows
- Recommendations from trusted sources carry weight
- Give things 2-3 episodes before deciding
- Rewatching favorites is valid
