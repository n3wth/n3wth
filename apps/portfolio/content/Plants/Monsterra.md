# Monstera deliciosa

The iconic Swiss cheese plant, known for its dramatic fenestrated leaves. A centerpiece of my indoor garden.

## Care Log

**May 2025**: Repotted using [[Indoor Soil Mix]] - showing new growth within 2 weeks.

## Care Requirements

**Light**: Bright indirect light. Direct sun burns the leaves; too little light stunts fenestration development.

**Water**: When top 2-3 inches of soil are dry. Yellowing leaves = overwatering. Drooping = underwatering.

**Humidity**: Appreciates 60%+ humidity but tolerates normal household levels.

**Soil**: Well-draining mix - see [[Indoor Soil Mix]].

## Growth Notes

- New leaves emerge from the central growth point
- Fenestrations (holes) develop as the plant matures
- Aerial roots can be trained to climb a moss pole
- Propagates easily from stem cuttings with nodes
