# Companies

A collection of organizations worth knowing—employers, potential targets, industry leaders, and companies doing interesting work.

## Categories

**Current/Past Employers**
Direct experience and insider knowledge. These shape my understanding of how organizations actually work.

**Target Companies**
Organizations that align with my [[Career Development]] goals. Worth understanding deeply for potential opportunities.

**Industry Leaders**
Companies setting the pace in relevant sectors. Useful for understanding trends and best practices.

**Interesting Startups**
Early-stage companies doing novel work. Often sources of innovation and alternative approaches.

## Research Framework

When evaluating a company, consider:

- **Mission & Values**: What do they claim to care about? What do they actually prioritize?
- **Culture signals**: Glassdoor, LinkedIn, employee testimonials
- **Growth trajectory**: Is this a scaling, stable, or declining organization?
- **Tech stack & practices**: For tech roles—what are they building with?
- **Leadership**: Who's running things? What's their track record?
