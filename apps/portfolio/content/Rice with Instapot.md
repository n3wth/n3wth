# Rice with Instapot

The Instant Pot makes foolproof rice. The key is the 1:1 ratio and proper rinsing—this method works for white rice every time.

## The Basic Method

**Ratio**: 1:1 rice to water by weight or volume

**Steps**:

1. **Rinse** the rice in cold water until it runs clear (usually 3-4 rinses)
2. **Add** equal parts rice and water to the pot
3. **Seal** the lid and set valve to sealing position
4. **Cook** on Manual/Pressure Cook HIGH for 3-4 minutes
5. **Natural release** for 10-15 minutes, then quick release remaining pressure
6. **Fluff** with a fork before serving

## Tips for Perfect Rice

- **Rinsing is essential**: Removes excess starch that causes gummy texture
- **Don't open early**: Let the natural release do its work
- **Rest after opening**: A few minutes of resting improves texture
- **Scale proportionally**: Double the rice, double the water

## Variations

| Rice Type  | Water Ratio | Cook Time | Natural Release |
| ---------- | ----------- | --------- | --------------- |
| White rice | 1:1         | 3-4 min   | 10 min          |
| Jasmine    | 1:1         | 3 min     | 10 min          |
| Brown rice | 1:1.25      | 22 min    | 10 min          |
| Basmati    | 1:1         | 4 min     | 10 min          |
