Comprehensive strategies for preventing and managing acne through consistent skincare practices and lifestyle modifications.

## Core Strategies

### Skincare Routine

- Gentle cleansing
- Non-comedogenic products
- Regular exfoliation

### Diet and Lifestyle

- Balanced diet
- Hydration
- Stress management

### Medical Treatments

- Topical treatments (retinoids, benzoyl peroxide)
- Oral medications (when appropriate)
- Professional dermatology consultations

## Application

1. **Daily Routine**
   - Consistent skincare
   - Healthy habits
   - Stress reduction

2. **Treatment Plans**
   - Dermatologist guidance
   - Personalized care
   - Regular monitoring

## Best Practices

- Avoid harsh products that strip natural oils
- Maintain consistency in skincare routine
- Seek professional advice for persistent acne
- Use [[non-comedogenic]] products
