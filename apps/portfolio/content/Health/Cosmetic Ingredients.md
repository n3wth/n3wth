Understanding the key components and active ingredients in skincare and beauty products to make informed choices for optimal skin health.

## Core Categories

### Emollients

- Moisturizing agents
- Skin softening
- Barrier protection

### Preservatives

- Product stability
- Microbial prevention
- Shelf life extension

### Active Ingredients

- Targeted treatments
- Anti-aging
- Acne control

## Application

1. **Product Selection**
   - Ingredient awareness
   - Skin compatibility
   - Desired effects

2. **Skincare Routine**
   - Product layering
   - Sensitivity management
   - Consistent use

## Common Ingredients to Know

### Beneficial Ingredients

- **Hyaluronic Acid** - Intense hydration
- **Retinoids** - Anti-aging and cell turnover
- **Niacinamide** - Reduces inflammation
- **Vitamin C** - Brightening and antioxidant
- **Salicylic Acid** - Exfoliation and acne treatment

### Potentially Irritating

- **Fragrances** - Common allergens
- **Alcohol (denatured)** - Can be drying
- **Essential Oils** - May cause sensitivity
- **Sulfates** - Can strip natural oils

## Best Practices

- Read labels carefully before purchasing
- Patch test new products on small area first
- Introduce one new product at a time
- Consult dermatologists for specific concerns
