# Alaska Subscriptions

Tracking fare sales and deals for Alaska Airlines routes—particularly useful for Pacific Northwest travel and trips to Hawaii, Mexico, and the West Coast.

## Why Alaska Airlines?

- **Mileage Plan**: One of the best frequent flyer programs for earning and redeeming miles
- **Partner network**: Access to oneworld alliance + unique partners like Emirates
- **West Coast hub**: Best options from Seattle, Portland, San Francisco
- **Credit card synergy**: Good earning rates with Bank of America cards

## Deal Sources

**Email Alerts**

- Alaska Airlines fare sales newsletter
- Scott's Cheap Flights (now Going) for error fares
- Secret Flying for mistake fares

**Social Media**

- @ThePointsGuy for credit card + airline deals
- @AlaskaAir for official announcements

## Key Routes to Watch

- Seattle ↔ Hawaii (watch for $99-199 each way sales)
- West Coast ↔ Mexico (Cabo, Puerto Vallarta)
- Seattle ↔ Alaska (Juneau, Anchorage, Ketchikan)
