# Hotel Loyalty & Points Optimization

Hotel point valuations vary widely. Focusing on high-value transfer partners provides significantly higher out-of-pocket savings compared to booking cash or fixed-rate travel portals.

## World of Hyatt: Primary Redemption Engine

World of Hyatt remains the premier transfer target for flexible points from Chase Ultimate Rewards and Bilt Rewards due to its fixed category award chart.

- **Transfer Ratio**: 1:1 instant transfers from Chase UR and Bilt.
- **Valuation**: Regularly achieves 1.8 to 2.5+ cents per point (cpp) value.

### Key Redemption Sweet Spots

- **Southeast Asia**: Properties like Park Hyatt Saigon at 25,000–30,000 points per night yield exceptional CPP against cash rates.
- **European Capitals**: Premium properties in London, Paris, and Amsterdam offer outsized savings during peak travel seasons.

## Points Splicing Strategy

When point balances are split across accounts, combine Bilt Points and Chase Ultimate Rewards toward a single Hyatt redemption goal:

1. **Calculate Required Coverage**: Determine total points needed for a multi-night stay.
2. **Execute Dual 1:1 Transfers**: Transfer Bilt points and Chase UR points simultaneously to the same World of Hyatt member account.
3. **Out-of-Pocket Result**: A 80,000–85,000 point balance cover 3–4 nights of luxury lodging, saving over $1,200 in cash outlay.

## General Rules for Hotel Redemptions

- Avoid fixed-value portal bookings (e.g., 1.0–1.25 cpp portal rates) when 1:1 transfers to World of Hyatt yield >1.8 cpp.
- Factor in resort fee waivers and free breakfast benefits on award stays for elite status holders.
