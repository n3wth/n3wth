# Board Games

Tabletop gaming is one of my favorite social activities—bringing people together for strategic thinking, storytelling, and good-natured competition.

## Why Board Games?

- **Unplugged connection**: Face-to-face interaction without screens
- **Mental exercise**: Strategy, planning, and adaptation
- **Shared experience**: Creating memorable moments with friends
- **Variety**: Different games for different moods and group sizes

## Favorite Categories

**Strategy Games**
Deep thinking, resource management, long-term planning. Best with 2-4 players who want to engage fully.

**Party Games**
Light, social, and hilarious. Perfect for larger groups and mixed experience levels.

**Cooperative Games**
Working together against the game itself. Great for building teamwork without competition stress.

**Engine Builders**
Creating systems that grow more powerful over time. Satisfying progression loops.

## Game Night Philosophy

- Match the game to the group (not vice versa)
- Teach patiently—everyone was a beginner once
- Winning matters less than the experience
- Keep a variety of lengths and complexity levels
