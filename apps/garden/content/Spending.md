# Spending

Understanding where money goes is the first step to making intentional financial decisions. This page collects spending analyses and insights.

## Why Track Spending?

- **Awareness**: Hard to change what you don't measure
- **Patterns**: Recurring expenses add up faster than one-time purchases
- **Trade-offs**: Every dollar spent is a choice about priorities
- **Forecasting**: Historical data helps predict future needs

## Spending Philosophy

1. **Spend on experiences over things** (usually)
2. **Buy quality for frequently-used items**
3. **Question subscriptions regularly**
4. **Automate savings before spending**

## Categories Worth Watching

**Often underestimated**:

- Subscriptions (streaming, SaaS, memberships)
- Food delivery and eating out
- Small recurring charges

**Often overestimated**:

- One-time purchases
- Entertainment
