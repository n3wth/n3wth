Evidence-based strategies for maintaining health, vitality, and quality of life throughout the aging process.

## Key Areas

### Physical Activity

- Regular exercise tailored to current ability
- Focus on strength, balance, and flexibility
- Adapt activities as needs change

### Nutrition

- Balanced diet with nutrient-dense foods
- Adequate protein for muscle maintenance
- Hydration awareness

### Mental Stimulation

- Lifelong learning and cognitive challenges
- New skills and hobbies
- Mental exercises and puzzles

### Social Connections

- Maintaining relationships and community involvement
- Regular social activities
- Intergenerational connections

## Preventive Care

- Regular health check-ups and age-appropriate screenings
- Proactive medication management
- Fall prevention strategies and home safety
- Bone density monitoring
- Vision and hearing assessments

## Emotional Well-being

- Stress management techniques adapted for life stage
- Cultivating purpose and meaning in later years
- Adapting to life transitions gracefully
- Managing grief and loss
- Maintaining optimism and resilience
