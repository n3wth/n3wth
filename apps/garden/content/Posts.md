# Posts

Writing intended for public consumption—blog posts, articles, and essays that share ideas beyond the personal notes in this garden.

## The Difference: Post vs Note

**Notes** are for me—quick captures, working documents, evolving thoughts.

**Posts** are for others—polished, structured, with a beginning and end.

The digital garden philosophy suggests these boundaries are blurry. Some notes grow into posts. Some posts link back to notes. That's fine.

## Post Lifecycle

1. **Idea capture**: Spark recorded in notes
2. **Draft**: Rough structure, main points
3. **Review**: Edit for clarity and flow
4. **Publish**: Share with the world
5. **Update**: Posts can evolve—this isn't print

## Writing Philosophy

- [[Write like no-ones watching|Write like no one's watching]] in drafts
- Edit assuming a curious reader
- Prefer clarity over cleverness
- Link generously—both internal and external
- Done is better than perfect
