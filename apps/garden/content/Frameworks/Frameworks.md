## How to Use Frameworks

1. **Select the Right Tool:** Choose frameworks based on your specific challenge
2. **Apply Systematically:** Follow the framework structure consistently
3. **Adapt as Needed:** Modify frameworks to fit your context
4. **Combine When Useful:** Use multiple frameworks together for complex problems
5. **Practice Regularly:** Build familiarity through repeated use
