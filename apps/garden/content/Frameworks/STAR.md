STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) is a structured approach for answering behavioral interview questions.

## Components

- **Situation:** Set the context
- **Task:** Describe your responsibility
- **Action:** Explain what you did
- **Result:** Share the outcome

## Usage

Used for behavioral interview questions to provide clear, structured responses that demonstrate your experience and impact.

## Examples and Applications

- **[[Interview Preparation]]:** Structure responses to behavioral questions
- **Performance Reviews:** Present achievements clearly
- **Storytelling:** Communicate experiences effectively
