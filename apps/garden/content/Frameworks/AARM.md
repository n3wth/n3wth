AARM (Acquire, Activate, Retain, Monetize) is a framework for product strategy and growth.

## Components

- **Acquire:** Attract new users
- **Activate:** Engage users with core value
- **Retain:** Keep users coming back
- **Monetize:** Generate revenue from users

## Usage

Used for strategic planning around user growth and revenue generation. Helps prioritize product initiatives across the user lifecycle.
