The Eisenhower Matrix is a framework for prioritizing tasks based on urgency and importance, helping focus on what truly matters.

## Components

- **Urgent & Important:** Do immediately
- **Important, Not Urgent:** Schedule
- **Urgent, Not Important:** Delegate
- **Neither Urgent Nor Important:** Eliminate

## Usage

Used for time management and task prioritization. Helps distinguish between what's truly important versus merely urgent.

## Examples and Applications

- **Daily Planning:** Sort tasks into quadrants each morning
- **Project Management:** Prioritize features and deadlines
- **Personal Productivity:** Focus on high-impact activities
