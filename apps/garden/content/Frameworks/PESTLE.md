PESTLE Analysis is a framework for analyzing macro-environmental factors that might impact an organization or product.

## Components

- **Political:** Government policies and stability
- **Economic:** Economic factors and trends
- **Social:** Cultural and demographic factors
- **Technological:** Tech innovations and trends
- **Legal:** Laws and regulations
- **Environmental:** Environmental concerns and impacts

## Usage

Used for strategic planning and market analysis. Helps identify external factors that could impact business success.

## Examples and Applications

- **Market Entry:** Analyze new market conditions
- **Strategic Planning:** Identify opportunities and threats
- **Risk Assessment:** Understand external business risks
