SCQA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer) is a framework for structured communication that helps present information clearly and persuasively.

## Components

- **Situation:** Current context
- **Complication:** What changed?
- **Question:** What needs to be addressed?
- **Answer:** Proposed solution

## Usage

Used for clear and effective communication. Particularly useful for presentations, proposals, and executive briefings.

## Examples and Applications

- **Business Presentations:** Structure compelling narratives
- **Problem Statements:** Clearly articulate issues and solutions
- **Executive Communication:** Present recommendations effectively
