## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **System overwhelm** - Trying too many at once
- **Perfectionism** - Spending too much time on the system
- **Rigidity** - Not adapting to changing needs
- **Tool obsession** - Focusing on tools over outcomes

> [!tip] Productivity Truth
> The best productivity system is the one you actually use. Start simple, stay consistent, and evolve based on what works for you.
