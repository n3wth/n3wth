### Monthly Reflection

- What was my biggest win this month?

- What patterns do I see in my progress?
- What support do I need?
- How has my career vision evolved?

- How have I grown professionally?
- What opportunities did I miss and why?
- What's my biggest lesson learned?
- How will next year be different?
