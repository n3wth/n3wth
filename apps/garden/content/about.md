# About

Notes I keep on things I'm learning. Some are rough, some are polished. They link to each other; following the links is half the point.

## The gardener

Oliver Newth - AI Product Leader. [n3wth.com](https://n3wth.com)

## How notes grow here

Every note carries a growth stage, and the site draws each one as a small plant - its height follows the stage, its branches follow how many other notes it touches:

- **Seedling** - a fresh planting: rough, unfinished, likely to change
- **Budding** - taking shape: structured, linked, still tended often
- **Evergreen** - mature and stable: revisited when something changes

Notes also show when they were planted (first written) and last tended (most recently edited). The [[Note Status Guide]] documents the vault's original seed → seedling → evergreen taxonomy, which the site maps onto the three stages above.
