# Indoor Soil Mix

A well-draining, nutrient-rich soil mix is essential for indoor plant health. Unlike outdoor gardens, indoor plants can't rely on natural soil ecosystems—you need to create the right environment.

## The Base Recipe

**Standard Mix (3:1:1 ratio)**

- 3 parts quality potting soil
- 1 part perlite (for drainage)
- 1 part orchid bark or coco coir (for aeration)

**For Tropical Plants**
Add extra organic matter and retain more moisture. Reduce perlite slightly.

**For Succulents & Cacti**
Increase perlite to 2 parts, add coarse sand. These need excellent drainage.

## Key Principles

- **Drainage is non-negotiable**: Soggy roots = dead plants
- **Refresh annually**: Soil compacts and loses nutrients over time
- **Match to plant needs**: Aroids want chunky, succulents want gritty

## Signs Your Mix Needs Work

- Water pools on surface → needs more perlite
- Dries too fast → needs more organic matter
- Root rot appearing → drainage issue
