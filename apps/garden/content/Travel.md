## Key Principles

1. **Keep Flexible Points Liquid**: Do not transfer Chase UR, Bilt, or Amex MR points to partner programs until an award seat is confirmed available.
2. **Prioritize High-CPP Transfers**: Target redemptions yielding >2.0 cents per point (cpp) for flights and >1.8 cpp for hotel stays.
3. **Airport Efficiency**: Optimize for origin/destination airports with lower friction (e.g., preference for BUR over LAX on West Coast routes).
