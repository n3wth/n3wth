## Overview

Spaced Repetition is a learning technique that involves reviewing material at increasing intervals to optimize long-term retention and memory consolidation.

## Core Principles

### The Forgetting Curve

- Memory naturally decays over time
- Regular review resets the forgetting curve
- Optimal intervals maximize retention efficiency

### Review Schedule

1. Initial learning
2. Review after 1 day
3. Review after 3 days
4. Review after 1 week
5. Review after 2 weeks
6. Review after 1 month
7. Review after 3 months

## Benefits

- Improved long-term retention
- Efficient use of study time
- Reduced forgetting curve
- Better knowledge consolidation
- Active recall practice
- Metacognitive awareness

## Application Areas

### Academic Learning

- Language acquisition
- Medical terminology
- Scientific concepts
- Historical facts

### Professional Development

- Technical concepts
- Industry knowledge
- Certification preparation
- Skill reinforcement

## Best Practices

- Create clear, concise cards
- Focus on understanding, not memorization
- Review consistently
- Adjust intervals based on difficulty
- Combine with other learning techniques
