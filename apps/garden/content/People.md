# People

A personal index of people worth remembering—mentors, colleagues, thinkers, and anyone whose ideas or influence shape my perspective.

## Why Keep Notes on People?

- **Context for conversations**: Remember past discussions and shared interests
- **Network nurturing**: Don't forget meaningful connections
- **Intellectual influences**: Track whose ideas shape your thinking
- **Gratitude practice**: Recognize those who've helped you grow

## Categories

**Mentors & Advisors**
People who've directly shaped my career and thinking.

**Colleagues & Collaborators**
People I work with or have worked with closely.

**Thinkers & Influences**
Authors, speakers, and creators whose ideas I follow.

**Personal Connections**
Friends and family worth noting.

## Note-Taking Approach

For each person:

- How we met / how I discovered them
- Key ideas or contributions
- Notable interactions or lessons
- How to stay connected
