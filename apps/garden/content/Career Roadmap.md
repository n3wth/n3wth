## Career Planning

### Short-Term Goals (0-2 years)

- Skill development
- Role mastery
- Network building
- Quick wins

### Medium-Term Goals (2-5 years)

- Role advancement
- [[Leadership Development|Leadership development]]
- Industry expertise
- Professional recognition

### Long-Term Goals (5+ years)

- Career transitions
- Executive positions
- Industry influence
- Legacy building
