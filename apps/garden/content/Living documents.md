# Living Documents

Living documents are dynamic notes that evolve over time, continuously updated to reflect current understanding. Unlike static blog posts or finished articles, they embrace impermanence and growth.

## Core Principles

**Continuous Refinement**

- Start with rough ideas, refine as understanding deepens
- No note is ever truly "finished"
- Each revision adds layers of insight

**Small, Atomic Thoughts**
Following the [[Zettelkasten Method]], each document should contain one clear idea that can be linked to others.

**Transparency About State**

- Mark notes with status (seedling, growing, evergreen)
- Date modifications to show evolution
- Acknowledge gaps and uncertainties

## Why This Matters

Traditional writing creates pressure for perfection. Living documents remove that barrier by embracing the garden metaphor—ideas are planted, tended, and grown rather than manufactured and shipped.

This approach supports:

- [[Active Learning]] through iterative engagement
- [[Knowledge Organization]] that evolves naturally
- [[Digital Gardening]] as a sustainable practice
